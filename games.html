// Something for the break room. Free to play in your browser, on a phone or a computer, with nothing to install.

> Hospital Kombat Pick a specialty and fight. Nine fighters, five hospital stages, and a signature Final Procedure for each.

How to play Hospital Kombat

  • Arcade: beat the other eight specialties one at a time. Each rival hits harder and reacts faster, and your best score is saved on your device.
  • Same keyboard: two players on one computer.
  • Final Procedure: once your rival drops below 25% health, land it up close to win the round on the spot. It can't be blocked, but a miss leaves you wide open.
  • On a phone: slide your thumb on the round pad to move and use the big buttons to attack. Turn the phone sideways for a bigger arena.
