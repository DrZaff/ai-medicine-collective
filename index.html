// Featured creation

JungleCards

Spaced-repetition flashcards for medicine, built by Brandon Zaffuto.

Anki is powerful, but you find, download and tidy decks yourself, and set it up on every device. JungleCards keeps what works, the spaced repetition and the fill-in-the-blank cards, and removes the chores.

  • One shared, checked library. Cards are written once for everyone and reviewed by a moderator before they go live, so there are no decks to hunt for.
  • Study by topic. Pick a topic and its subtopics come with it; mix new and due cards in a few taps.
  • AI-drafted cards. Paste your notes and get draft cards to edit, which a moderator checks before they join the library.
  • Simpler reviews. Three buttons, Difficult, Medium and Easy, and a dashboard that shows what is due and how you are doing.
  • Nothing to install or sync. It runs in the browser on any device, with your progress saved to your account.
