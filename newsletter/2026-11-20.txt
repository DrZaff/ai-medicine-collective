// THE ONE THING
Artificial intelligence–based clinical simulation in undergraduate medical education: contributions of virtual patients, chatbots, and immersive environments - a scoping review
Educators planning AI virtual-patient or chatbot practice for history-taking and communication can use this review to see what has already been tried. It also shows that immersive AI simulation has much less evidence behind it so far.
https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s12909-026-10793-8

// AI VS PHYSICIAN
This week: AI helped improve
Dermoscopy-Based AI Risk Scoring Enhanced Experienced Dermatologists' Decision-Making: a Large Retrospective Reader Study
The biggest gain was in catching melanoma, even among experienced dermatologists, which makes this a useful example of measuring AI by how it changes clinicians' decisions rather than by the model's accuracy alone. Learners should note that a before-and-after reader study on images does not show how the tool would perform with real patients.
https://www.dpcj.org/index.php/dpc/article/view/6981
