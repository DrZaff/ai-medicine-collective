// THE ONE THING
Exploring prompt literacy in allied health professions education: an explanatory sequential mixed-methods study of student and educator perspectives
Most learners are using AI without any formal training. This study offers a practical breakdown of the skills that curricula and faculty development could build step by step, and it applies to medical programmes as well as allied health.
https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s12909-026-10829-z

// AI VS PHYSICIAN
This week: Physicians outperformed AI
Diagnostic Accuracy of Large Language Models for Pediatric Chest Radiograph: Comparison With Pediatricians and Radiologists
Trainees who upload images to general-purpose chatbots should know that these tools gave only fair screening and fell off sharply on specific diagnoses. Pediatricians did better on most measures in this study.
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43033194
