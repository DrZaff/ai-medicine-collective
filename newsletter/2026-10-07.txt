// THE ONE THING
Machine Learning for Human-Autonomy Teaming in Surgical Skill Assessment: Scoping Review
Surgical educators being pitched automated skill scoring should ask for external validation and evidence from real settings first, because most tools have only been tested on benchmark data. Residents should treat AI-generated skill scores as experimental, not as an established assessment.
https://ai.jmir.org/2026/1/e94109
