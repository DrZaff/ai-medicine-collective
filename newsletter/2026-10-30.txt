// THE ONE THING
Medical students' knowledge, attitudes, and practices regarding artificial intelligence: A cross-sectional study
Students are already using AI, but this study suggests many do not yet understand it well. That supports teaching AI literacy explicitly in the curriculum rather than assuming students will pick it up on their own.
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42945317/

// AI VS PHYSICIAN
This week: Mixed results
Clinicians vs. artificial intelligence in patient outcome prediction in the intensive care unit
How a clinician compared with the AI depended on their experience, which is useful context for the debate over trainees relying on AI. For educators, it supports teaching learners to combine their own judgement with model output instead of deferring to it.
https://www.nature.com/articles/s41746-026-03132-0
