AI vs physician

Dermoscopy-Based AI Risk Scoring Enhanced Experienced Dermatologists' Decision-Making: a Large Retrospective Reader Study

This week’s result: AI helped improve.

The biggest gain was in catching melanoma, even among experienced dermatologists, which makes this a useful example of measuring AI by how it changes clinicians' decisions rather than by the model's accuracy alone. Learners should note that a before-and-after reader study on images does not show how the tool would perform with real patients.
