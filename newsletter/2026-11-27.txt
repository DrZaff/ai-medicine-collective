// AI VS PHYSICIAN
This week: Mixed results
Evaluating Human and AI-Based Triage Performance in the Emergency Department: Real-World Cohort Study
How AI compares with clinicians here depends on the task and on which clinician you compare it with. The models came close on urgency but lagged on specialty assignment, and the authors say they belong in a supporting, human-in-the-loop role. That is a useful case for teaching learners when to lean on an AI tool and when to override it.
https://www.jmir.org/2026/1/e91442
