AI vs physician

Deep Learning–Assisted Diagnosis of Middle Ear Diseases in Otoendoscopy Across Clinician Experience Levels: Retrospective Reader Study

This week’s result: AI helped improve.

In this reader study, AI support helped trainees most, which points to a possible use in otoscopy teaching. Learners should still keep training their own image-reading skills: a news report on the study says that 3.5% of initially correct readings became wrong after the clinicians consulted the AI.
