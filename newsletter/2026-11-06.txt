// THE ONE THING
Generative AI Factors, Cognitive Engagement, and Patient Safety Learning in Medical Education: Mixed Methods Study
The authors suggest clerkship guidance should cover supervised checking of AI output, protecting patient data, following local protocols, and keeping clinicians responsible for clinical decisions. Educators should treat this as a hypothesis to test, not proof that AI training improves patient safety.
https://mededu.jmir.org/2026/1/e100828

// AI VS PHYSICIAN
This week: AI help: no difference
From dusk till dawn?—Night shift-induced fatigue, diagnostic performance, and AI assistance in chest radiography
A tired resident's accuracy can drop while reading speed stays the same, so neither the resident nor a supervisor may notice. This study suggests that a pre-filled AI report alone does not fix the problem. Educators and programme leads should not count on AI as a substitute for fatigue management and supervision overnight.
https://doi.org/10.1007/s00330-026-12969-8
