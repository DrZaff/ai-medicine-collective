// THE ONE THING
Generative AI-related academic misconduct among medical students: co-occurrence with conventional misconduct and associated factors
Integrity policies and assessments built around older forms of cheating may miss a group of students whose only misconduct involves AI. Educators may need clear rules on disclosing AI use and assessment formats that take generative AI into account.
https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/public-health/articles/10.3389/fpubh.2026.2010823/full

// AI VS PHYSICIAN
This week: AI helped improve
Deep Learning–Assisted Diagnosis of Middle Ear Diseases in Otoendoscopy Across Clinician Experience Levels: Retrospective Reader Study
In this reader study, AI support helped trainees most, which points to a possible use in otoscopy teaching. Learners should still keep training their own image-reading skills: a news report on the study says that 3.5% of initially correct readings became wrong after the clinicians consulted the AI.
https://medinform.jmir.org/2026/1/e96134
