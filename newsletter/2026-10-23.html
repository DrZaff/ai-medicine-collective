AI vs physician

AI-based clinician decision support system for diagnosis of inherited retinal diseases: a multicenter, randomized trial

This week’s result: AI helped improve.

This is one of the few randomised trials of AI used alongside specialists in real patients, and it shows the biggest gains in a rare-disease field where expertise is scarce. Trainees should note that it tested AI as an aid to specialists, not as a replacement, and that the evidence on management benefit is still exploratory.

Radiologists, LLMs fail at spotting AI-generated chest x-rays

This week’s result: mixed results.

The models and the readers made different kinds of mistakes, so having one check the other does not reliably catch fake images. The authors suggest that tracking where an image came from at the point it is created, for example with watermarking, should be the main safeguard, with visual review as a backup.
