// THE ONE THING
Comparative Cost of Care Recommended by Large Language Models versus Treating Physicians
Trainees who use LLMs to draft plans should watch for over-ordering, because a plan can sound thorough and still add cost with no clear benefit. Educators can use this study to teach high-value care alongside AI literacy.
https://ai.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/AIoa2600537

// AI VS PHYSICIAN
This week: AI helped improve
AI-based clinician decision support system for diagnosis of inherited retinal diseases: a multicenter, randomized trial
This is one of the few randomised trials of AI used alongside specialists in real patients, and it shows the biggest gains in a rare-disease field where expertise is scarce. Trainees should note that it tested AI as an aid to specialists, not as a replacement, and that the evidence on management benefit is still exploratory.
https://www.nature.com/articles/s41591-026-04545-w
