They do not all agree, on purpose: the size of AI's footprint is still argued over, so watch more than one and check the figures against their sources. The Collective has not checked the claims made in them.

// Courses

Longer, structured learning. Check each provider's page for current cost and time.

  • Artificial Intelligence in Health Care Series American Medical Association (ChangeMedEd) with the University of Michigan

    A seven-part module series introducing AI and machine learning concepts in health care. Written for medical students and suitable for residents and faculty. Offered at no cost on the AMA Ed Hub.

  • AI in Healthcare Specialization Stanford University, on Coursera

    A multi-course series for clinicians and engineers on how AI is built on health data and how to judge whether it is safe to use.

  • AI for Medicine Specialization DeepLearning.AI, on Coursera

    A more technical, hands-on series on machine learning for diagnosis, prognosis and treatment. Assumes some programming.

  • AI For Everyone DeepLearning.AI, on Coursera

    A non-technical course on what AI can and cannot do. A good choice for people who lead or teach.

  • Elements of AI University of Helsinki and MinnaLearn

    A free online course on the basics of AI for people with no technical background.

// Guidance, policy and evidence

What the major bodies say, and where the research is published.

  • Principles for the Responsible Use of Artificial Intelligence in and for Medical Education AAMC

    The Association of American Medical Colleges' principles for schools and programs bringing AI into teaching.

  • Ethics and governance of artificial intelligence for health: guidance on large multi-modal models World Health Organization

    WHO guidance on the risks and governance of large multi-modal models in health care.

  • List of Artificial Intelligence-Enabled Medical Devices U.S. Food and Drug Administration

    The FDA's running list of AI-enabled devices it has authorized. Useful for checking whether a product is cleared.

  • NEJM AI NEJM Group

    A journal of AI in clinical medicine, from the publisher of the New England Journal of Medicine.
