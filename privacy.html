PRIVACY POLICY

// Last updated: November 8, 2026

The AI Medicine Collective is an independent, volunteer-run group of clinicians and trainees who build and share AI tools for medicine. This page explains what this website collects, who can see it, how long we keep it, and what you can do about it. It covers www.aimedicinecollective.com and the same site at ai-medicine-collective.netlify.app. The rules for using the site are on the terms of use page.

// The short version

  • We collect what you give us to join and take part, and nothing to sell or advertise with.
  • Tools and materials you publish are public, with your name and photo. Almost everything else is for approved members only.
  • Your sign-in email address is never shown to other members.
  • Some emails and your listing in the member list are on by default; you can switch each one off on your account page.
  • No advertising, no analytics trackers, no selling your information, and no training AI models on it.
  • Never post patient information anywhere on this site.

// What we collect, and who can see it

  • Your account. Your email address, name and, if you sign in with Google, your Google profile photo. If you choose email and a password, your password is stored by our sign-in provider, Supabase, in scrambled form; we cannot see it. Your email address is visible only to you and to the Collective's moderators, who use it to manage memberships.
  • Your membership request. Your role, program, interests and any message. Only you and the moderators can see it.
  • Your member profile. If you fill it in: program, training level, institution, focus areas, a short bio, and an optional contact address and link. Approved members can see it.
  • The member list. Approved members appear in the member list, which only other approved members can see. This is on by default; untick "Show me in the member list" under "Your profile" on your account page to leave it. Your profile still opens from things you have posted.
  • Tools and learning materials you publish. Once a moderator publishes them, they are public: anyone on the internet can see the title, description, links and attached file, together with your name and profile photo. Items waiting for review are seen only by you and the moderators.
  • Comments, chat messages, tool requests and votes, and meeting votes. Visible to approved members, with your name. For meetings, other members see how many people ticked each time, not who.
  • Private messages. A message you send about a tool, and the replies, can be read only by the two of you. Either of you can delete a message. If one of you reports a message, a copy of it goes to the moderators with the report.
  • Reports. If you report something, the moderators see your report, your name and a copy of what you reported. The person you report is not told who reported them.
  • Bearcat Pinball high scores. If you submit a score, the nickname you type and your score are shown publicly on the game's leaderboard, with a random ID kept in your browser so each device has one entry. This works without an account and is not linked to one.
  • Basic technical data. Our hosting and database providers keep standard server logs (such as IP address, browser type and the time of a request) to keep the site running and secure.

// Emails we send

  • Sign-in codes when you create a password account or reset your password.
  • Private message alerts: a short notice, at most every 30 minutes, with a link to your inbox. The message itself is never included. On by default.
  • The weekly digest for approved members: briefings and news from the Collective. Each copy goes to one person. On by default.
  • Meeting confirmations, only if a time you voted for is confirmed.

Switch alerts and the digest off under "Your profile" on your account page. We do not track whether you open emails or click their links.

// Stored in your browser

The site keeps a few small things in your browser's storage: that you are signed in, a hint of your role and unread messages (to draw the menu before the page has loaded), your light or dark theme, course progress, the last tools you opened, and your Bearcat Pinball device ID. They are not cookies used for tracking, and except for signing in and the pinball ID, they are never sent to us. Clearing your browser's site data removes them.

// How we use it

  • To review membership requests and reply to you.
  • To let you sign in and show your name next to what you post.
  • To send the emails above.
  • To moderate content and keep the site safe.

Information from Google is used only to create and identify your account here. We do not sell your information, share it with advertisers, or use it to train AI models. The AI-written parts of the site (blog briefings and the AI vs Physician paper) are drafted from published research and are never given members' information.

// Services we rely on

These companies process data for us so the site can work. They are based in, or store data in, the United States.

  • Netlify: hosts the website.
  • Supabase: stores accounts, member content and uploaded files, and handles sign-in.
  • Google: provides sign-in, if you choose it.
  • Resend: delivers the emails we send.
  • Formspree: emails our organizers when a new membership request arrives.
  • Cloudflare: runs a short automated check on the email sign-in forms to stop bots.
  • jsDelivr: delivers the code library the member pages use to talk to Supabase.
  • YouTube (Google): plays videos on the Learning page; nothing loads from YouTube until you press play.
  • Instagram (Meta): we post our public briefings to @aimedicinecollective. We send Instagram only the post's text and pictures, never members' information.

// No patient information

Never submit patient information anywhere on this site: no names, dates, record numbers, images, or case details that could identify a patient. The Collective does not hold patient records and is not a health care provider. Moderators remove patient information as soon as they find it or it is reported; see the terms of use.

// How long we keep it

  • Your account and profile: while you are a member, until you ask us to delete them.
  • What you post: until you or a moderator deletes it, or your account is deleted.
  • Declined membership requests: up to a year, so a moderator can see an earlier request.
  • Reports: a copy of the reported text is kept until the report is closed.
  • Server logs: kept by our providers for their usual periods, typically days to weeks.

// Your choices and rights

  • You can ask us to show you, correct, export or delete your information, including your whole account and what you have posted, by emailing the address below. We answer within 30 days. Wherever you live, we give everyone the rights that privacy laws such as California's CCPA and Europe's GDPR provide.
  • You can edit your profile and delete your own posts at any time.
  • You can remove this site's access to your Google account in your Google Account settings, under third-party connections.

// Who this site is for

Membership is for adults (18 or older) who work or train in health care or a related field. The site is not meant for children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

// Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top, and tell members about important changes in the weekly digest. Questions or requests: zaffutbn@ucmail.uc.edu.
