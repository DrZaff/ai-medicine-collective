Generative AI in medical training, and what source-linked AI advice does to physicians' answers

A JMIR scoping review maps how generative AI is being used in medical education and proposes a road map for building it into teaching. A new preprint shows why that teaching needs to cover how clinicians judge AI evidence: an AI assistant that cites its sources made physicians more accurate, but it also made them more likely to accept its wrong answers.
