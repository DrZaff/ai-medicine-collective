Residents learn ventilation support with AI simulation, and LLM care plans cost more than physicians' plans

In this briefing, an AI-assisted simulation boosted internal medicine residents' confidence with noninvasive respiratory support. Separately, a NEJM AI study found that care plans written by large language models ordered far more, and cost far more, than the plans treating physicians actually used. Both pieces are a reminder to judge AI by what it does to learning and to care, not only by whether it gets the answer right.
