Neurosurgical vignettes with ChatGPT, and an AI system that matches neurologists on autopsy-confirmed cases

Both reviews look at AI in diagnostic reasoning for brain and nervous-system disease. In the first, residents got more accurate with ChatGPT-4 on written neurosurgical emergencies. In the second, an LLM-based system was about as accurate as experienced neurologists on complex cases whose true diagnosis was confirmed at autopsy.
