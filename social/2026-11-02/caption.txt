ChatGPT lifts residents' decisions in neurosurgical emergencies, but only while it is open

In a simulation study, ChatGPT-4 raised trainees' accuracy on neurosurgical emergency cases, most of all for junior residents. The harder question for educators is what those trainees can still do once the tool is taken away.

Med-ed: AI-augmented decision support in neurosurgical emergencies: a prospective study of resident performance (BMC Medical Education)
