AI in the sim lab, and a rheumatology chatbot that saved time but not accuracy

A new scoping review maps how medical schools are using AI-driven virtual patients and chatbots for clinical simulation. A multicentre randomised trial offers a reality check: physicians given a certified LLM diagnostic assistant worked faster than colleagues without it, but were no more accurate.
