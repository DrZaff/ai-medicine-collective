Simulated patients, real gains: AI virtual cases for students and a live diagnostic copilot for family doctors

Both reviews use simulated patients to test AI. In one, AI virtual patient cases improved surgery students' exam and OSCE results. In the other, a real-time AI assistant helped experienced family physicians include the right diagnosis in their top three more often, though there were signs that doctors may lean too hard on its suggestions.
