TERMS OF USE

// Last updated: November 8, 2026

These terms apply to everyone who uses www.aimedicinecollective.com (and the same site at ai-medicine-collective.netlify.app). By using the site, or by joining, you agree to them. What we collect and how we use it is on the privacy policy page.

// Who we are

The AI Medicine Collective is an independent, volunteer-run group of clinicians and trainees who build and share AI tools for medicine. It is not a health care provider, a company, or part of any university or hospital, and it is not paid by anyone to recommend a product.

// Not medical advice

  • Everything on this site (tools, learning materials, blog briefings, the AI vs Physician paper, chat and comments) is for education only. It is not medical advice and does not create a doctor-patient relationship with anyone.
  • Some content is drafted by AI and reviewed by volunteers. It may be wrong, incomplete or out of date. Check everything against primary sources and use your own clinical judgment before acting on it.
  • Tools shared here are built by members, not tested or approved as medical devices. Do not rely on any of them as the only basis for a decision about a patient.
  • If you find a mistake, please tell us; see "Contact" below.

// Membership

  • Membership is for adults (18 or older) who work or train in health care or a related field.
  • Moderators approve each request and may decline one. Declining is not permanent: you can ask again with more detail.
  • Use your real name and keep your sign-in to yourself. You are responsible for what is posted from your account.
  • Moderators may remove content, hide a profile, or end a membership that breaks these terms.
  • You can leave at any time and ask us to delete your account; see the privacy policy.

// Community rules

  • No patient information, ever. No names, dates, record numbers, photos, images, or case details that could identify a patient, anywhere on the site, including private messages and uploaded files. Examples must be made up.
  • Be respectful. No harassment, threats, discrimination or personal attacks. Disagree with ideas, not people.
  • Be honest. Don't pretend to be someone else, or claim a role or qualification you don't have.
  • Be careful with clinical claims. Say where a claim comes from, and say when something is your opinion or untested.
  • No spam or advertising. Sharing your own tool is welcome; selling, recruiting, or posting the same thing again and again is not.
  • Respect confidentiality. Don't post your employer's confidential information, or anything you don't have the right to share.
  • Nothing unlawful or harmful. No malware, no attempts to get around the site's security, and no tools meant to cause harm.

Every member can report a message, comment, tool, request, private message or profile with its "Report" link. Reports go only to the moderators, and the person reported is not told who reported them. Patient information is removed as soon as moderators find it or it is reported.

// What you post

  • You keep ownership of what you post. By posting it, you let the Collective show it on this site (and, for published tools and materials, share a link to it, for example in the weekly digest), for as long as it is posted.
  • Only post what you have the right to share. If a tool or material uses someone else's work, say so and follow its licence.
  • Published tools and materials are public, with your name and photo. Comments, chat and requests are visible to approved members.
  • If you think something here infringes your copyright, email us with the page and the work, and we will look at it promptly.

// Outside links and tools

Many tools and links lead to other websites and services that we do not run. Their own terms and privacy policies apply there, and we are not responsible for them. Never enter patient information into an outside tool unless your institution has approved it for that use.
